v2.0.4 October 23, 2026

Canyon Stadium: eight lanes, seven rivals

Reach level 40 and you'll race deep in a slot canyon. 2.0.4 adds the toughest field yet, a bell that tells you when someone takes your rank, and a new way to start every race.

Canyon Stadium

  • A new eight-lane stadium with a terracotta track, for level 40 and up
  • Seven rivals on the line, the biggest field in the game
  • It downloads in the background as you get close, and stays ready after you restart the app

Racing

  • Warm up, then line up with the field before the gun
  • Bolder runner name tags during the race
  • Meadow is now your first race, and Forest starts at level 2

Notifications & Rankings

  • A bell on the Run tab shows rank changes and news, with a "while you were away" recap
  • Country flags next to every name on the leaderboards

Everything else

  • New runners get a suggested name to start with
  • The game lets you know when a new version is out
