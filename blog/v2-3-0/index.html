v2.3.0 November 13, 2026

Desert Stadium and national records

Reach race level 70 and you'll race in the Desert Stadium. 2.3.0 also gives every country its own records, with bonus XP when you set one, and lets you challenge any runner on the leaderboards.

Out now on iPhone. Android follows in a few days.

Desert Stadium

  • A huge eight-lane arena for race levels 70 to 79

National records

  • Your results show your rank in your country next to your world rank
  • Set a world record for double XP, or a national record for +50%
  • Every new record pays, even when you beat your own

Leaderboards

  • Find a runner by name and tap Challenge to race their PB
  • Switch any race board between World and your country

Racing

  • See how much XP you've earned today
  • The pace line now follows your real PB splits
  • Top rivals now run at real world-record pace
  • More natural start poses, and the first-person camera now looks into the bends
  • New sounds for records, PBs and level-ups
