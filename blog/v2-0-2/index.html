v2.0.2 October 7, 2026

Splits every 100m and a bell for the last lap

2.0.2 is all about knowing where you stand mid-race. You now get a split every 100m against your PB pace, a bell buzz when the last lap starts, and rank alerts that tell you who just passed you.

Racing

  • Races of 200m and up show a split every 100m, compared with your PB pace
  • The first split allows for the standing start, so it isn't judged against a flying start
  • A light tap tells you when you're ahead of your PB pace at a split
  • In races of 800m and longer, a strong triple buzz rings with 400m to go, like the bell for the last lap
  • The warm-up before a race now shows each swipe as a sweep of falling dots, left side first, then right

Rank alerts

  • When someone passes you on a leaderboard, the alert now names them and says how many others passed you too

Fixes

  • A paused race no longer heats up your phone, because the game nearly stops drawing while the pause card is open
