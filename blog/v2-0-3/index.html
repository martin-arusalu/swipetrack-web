Coastal Stadium: race by the sea

Reach level 30 and you'll race on the coast. 2.0.3 adds a seaside stadium, reworks XP so every race level pays off, and lets you pick km or miles.

Coastal Stadium

  • A new seven-lane stadium by the sea, for levels 30–39
  • It downloads in the background as you get close, so it's ready when you level up

Racing

  • Every lane now runs the full race distance
  • The race screen shows how many metres you've run

XP & results

  • Results show how many races until your next attribute level, and your total XP for the race
  • XP has been reworked: a bigger bonus for every race level, and more XP for sprints
  • XP boosts now show on the Run tab

Profile & Rankings

  • Choose km or miles in your profile
  • Rankings now focus on race distances

Faster start & Android

  • The game starts faster, with a new loading screen
  • On Android, Sign in with Google to keep your runner safe
