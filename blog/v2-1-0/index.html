Glacier Stadium: racing through the snow

Reach level 50 and you'll race on ice. 2.1.0 is a big one: a snowy new stadium, a race you can hear, your runner in any colour you like, and a streak that pays off in XP.

Glacier Stadium

  • A new eight-lane stadium by a glacier lake, for levels 50 to 59
  • Snow falls across the whole stadium and streaks past you at speed
  • The fog grows darker as night falls

Sound

  • The starter calls READY, SET, GO, and the pistol fires on GO
  • A bell rings for the last lap
  • Wind rises as you speed up, and you hear footsteps from you and your rivals

Racing

  • A new first-person camera you can turn on in Runner settings
  • From 1500m the race screen shows your pace instead of your speed

Your Runner & Streak

  • Pick any colour from a colour wheel, with one swatch for each part of your outfit
  • A wider range of skin tones
  • Your daily streak now builds an XP bonus, all the way up to double XP

Android

  • Rank alerts and news arrive as push notifications
  • Tapping a notification opens the race it's about
  • Haptics, just like on iPhone
