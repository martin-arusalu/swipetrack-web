Get SwipeTrack on Android

SwipeTrack is in early access on Android. Three quick steps and you're racing. Use the same Google account for all three steps: the one your Play Store uses.

  1. Join the tester group

    Open the group and tap "Join group".

    Join the group

  2. Accept the test invite

    Tap "Become a tester".

    Become a tester

  3. Install from Google Play

    Download SwipeTrack and start racing.

    Open Google Play
Play says the app isn't available? Access can take a few minutes to go through after step 2. Wait a bit and try step 3 again.
