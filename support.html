Quick fixes

Troubleshooting

Most issues clear up with one of these steps before you even need to email us.

🔄

Force-quit and reopen

Swipe up from the bottom of your iPhone, swipe SwipeTrack off the top, then relaunch. This fixes the majority of one-off glitches.

🆙

Update iOS & SwipeTrack

Make sure you're on the latest iOS and have the most recent App Store update. Many issues are addressed in patch releases.

🔁

Reinstall the App

If a problem persists, delete and reinstall SwipeTrack. Note: this will reset your local records and progression.

SwipeTrack requires a deliberate downward swipe (roughly 0.5 cm or more on most iPhones). Quick taps and short flicks are intentionally ignored to prevent accidental input. Make sure you're swiping downward, and that your screen protector or case isn't blocking the very edges of the display.
